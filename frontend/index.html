A two-way marketplace

Neither side waits for the other

Unlike Fiverr, both sides openly publish what they want. Platform owners publish services and pricing. Businesses publish campaigns and payment terms. Either side can search, contact, propose, negotiate and buy.

For businesses

Businesses find platform owners

  • Search platform listings with real audience analytics
  • Filter by audience, niche, country, service and pricing model
  • Buy a listed offer or request a custom proposal
  • Message the owner and fund the agreement through PromoSlot
For platform owners

Platform owners find businesses

  • Browse active campaigns with payment terms published upfront
  • Filter by niche, platform, payment type and budget
  • Apply and accept the listed conditions — or send a counter-offer
  • Begin work once funding is secured in escrow
Trust — our core advantage

PromoSlot protects the transaction. You control the deal.

PromoSlot is not the employer, agency or campaign decision-maker. We provide discovery, negotiation tools, deal documentation, secure payment, delivery tracking, evidence, reviews, dispute support and fraud prevention.

🔒

Fund protection

The business funds the agreed deal before work starts. The creator knows the money is secured. The business knows nothing is released until the agreed delivery conditions are met.

✅

Verified delivery

Platform owners submit proof: published-post links, screenshots, platform analytics, view & impression counts, referral sales, click results, livestream recordings, and live-duration confirmation.

💸

Structured payment release

Fixed payment after approved delivery. View-based payment after the measurement period. Monthly payment per placement period. Commission after verified conversions. Hybrid split across milestones.

⚖️

Dispute support

If either party claims non-fulfilment, PromoSlot reviews the accepted terms, content links, evidence, analytics, messages, deadlines and revisions. Decisions follow the agreed deliverables — not whether a brand disliked the commercial outcome.

📄

Delivery ≠ performance

A creator may guarantee a video, a 30-minute livestream, 30 days live, minimum impressions or placements. Unless included in a funded performance agreement, they never guarantee sales, virality or revenue.

🛡️

Fraud prevention

Audience analytics evidence on every listing, business verification, review history on both sides, and escrow on every transaction — so neither party works or pays on trust alone.

Simple pricing: 10% seller fee + 5% buyer protection fee

No subscriptions, no listing fees. Both fees are calculated on the agreed price and only apply to completed deals. On a £500 deal the business pays £525 at checkout, the platform owner receives £450, and PromoSlot's total take is £75.
