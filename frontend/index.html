How it works

From agreement to payout, every step stays clear.

Set the terms, fund the deal and follow delivery, evidence and payment in one place. PromoSlot verifies deliverables and evidence — it does not guarantee sales, virality or revenue unless explicitly included in the funded terms.

INTERACTIVE EXAMPLE Not a live transaction

PromoSlot reviews and approves submitted evidence against the agreed deliverables before payment is released. Commercial results such as sales, virality or revenue are only covered when explicitly included in the funded agreement. Read the Payment Protection terms →

What PromoSlot verifies

  • That submitted evidence matches the deliverables both parties accepted
  • Platform ownership, before a listing can transact
  • That funds are held before delivery is expected to begin
  • That a human reviewer, not an automated check, makes the delivery decision

What isn't guaranteed

  • Sales, sign-ups or revenue from a promotion
  • Views, reach or engagement beyond what the agreement specifies
  • The commercial judgement of either party
  • Outcomes affected by a third-party platform's own moderation or reach changes
Questions

Frequently asked

Who decides whether delivery was acceptable?

A PromoSlot reviewer checks the submitted evidence against the accepted terms. If something is missing, the platform owner is given the chance to resubmit before any decision closes the deal.

Can terms change after both sides approve?

Only by agreeing a new version. Any counter-offer is recorded, and funding is always tied to the version both parties last approved.

What happens if the work is never published?

The deal cannot be verified, and eligible funds are returned to the business under the accepted terms.

Does PromoSlot choose who works with whom?

No. Businesses and platform owners find each other in the Marketplace and agree their own terms. PromoSlot records the agreement and reviews delivery.

Payment Protection

Funds are held until delivery is verified.

PromoSlot holds the funded amount from the moment a deal is agreed, and only releases it once a reviewer has checked the submitted evidence against the accepted terms.

How the protection works

Six steps, the same for every deal

  1. 1

    Terms accepted by both sides

    Deliverables, dates, payment model and evidence requirements are fixed at the version both parties approved.

  2. 2

    The business funds the deal

    The payment plus the buyer protection fee is taken and held by PromoSlot. It does not reach the platform owner yet.

  3. 3

    Delivery begins

    The platform owner publishes the agreed work, knowing the payment is already funded.

  4. 4

    Evidence is submitted

    Links, screenshots and analytics are uploaded against the requirements agreed at the start.

  5. 5

    A reviewer checks the record

    A person compares the evidence to the accepted terms and records the decision, with a chance to resubmit if something is missing.

  6. 6

    Release or return

    Verified delivery releases funds to the platform owner less the seller fee. Where conditions were not met, eligible funds are returned to the business.

Evidence PromoSlot looks for

  • A link to the published post, video or send
  • Screenshots showing the content live and dated
  • Platform analytics covering the agreed measurement window
  • View, impression or click figures where the payment model depends on them
  • Confirmation the content stayed live for the agreed period

What's excluded

  • Commercial results — sales, sign-ups or revenue — unless explicitly written into the agreement
  • Work agreed or paid for outside PromoSlot
  • Changes a third-party platform makes to reach, moderation or availability
  • Requirements never recorded in the accepted terms
